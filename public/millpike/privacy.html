Mill & Pike

Privacy Policy

Mill & Pike: Village Tactics, for iPhone and Android. Effective 17 October 2026.

Mill & Pike is a single-player game that runs offline. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no account or sign-in, and it makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no purchases, no tracking and no crash-reporting services, and it does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Your saved games

Your progress and settings are saved on your phone and never leave it. We cannot see them and cannot restore them. Deleting the game deletes them. If your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google device backup) includes app data, that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and the fonts are served from this site. Like any web server, our hosting and its network provider (Cloudflare) may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP address, time and page requested, for security and to fix problems. We do not use them to identify you and do not share them.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to reply, and we delete the conversation when you ask.

Your rights

Because the game collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you have a question about your data, or if you live in a region with specific privacy rights (such as the EU, UK or California) and want to use them, email us and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes any of this, for example by adding optional cloud saves, we will update this page and the store privacy labels before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
