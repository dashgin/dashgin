Support

Something wrong, or a question the answers below don't cover? Email the developer. I read every message.

hello@dashgin.com

Is the game really free?

Yes. The whole game is free: all five chapters and the ending, with no ads, no timers and nothing to buy. Stars come only from winning.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The game plays fully offline and has no account. You never need a connection to play.

Where is my save? Can I move it to another phone?

Saves are kept on your phone only. There is no cloud sync in this version, so progress does not move between phones, and deleting the game deletes your saves.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and Android phones, in landscape or portrait (Settings > Orientation). iPad and Android tablets are not supported in version 1. The game is in English; Azerbaijani, Turkish and Russian are coming in the next update.

Reporting a bug

Email hello@dashgin.com with:

  • your phone model and its iOS or Android version,
  • the game version (shown in the store listing),
  • what you did and what happened, with a screenshot or screen recording if you can.

Deleting your data

The game keeps no data about you anywhere except on your phone, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything, delete the app. See the privacy policy.
