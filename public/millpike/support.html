Support

Something wrong, or a question the answers below don't cover? Email the developer. I read every message.

dashgin@praxis.az

I bought the full campaign. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open the game, tap the gear icon for Settings, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The purchase is free to restore.

What is free and what does the purchase unlock?

The first three in-game years of Act 1 are free, with no time limit and no ads. One purchase unlocks everything else: the rest of Act 1, Acts 2 and 3, every lord, all 8 charters and new campaigns with modifiers. There is nothing else to buy.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The game plays fully offline and has no account. You only need a connection to buy or restore the unlock.

Where is my save? Can I move it to another phone?

Saves are kept on your phone only. There is no cloud sync in this version, so progress does not move between phones, and deleting the game deletes your saves. The purchase does move: see Restore purchases above.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund steps.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and Android phones, in landscape or portrait (Settings > Orientation). iPad and Android tablets are not supported in version 1. The game is in English.

Reporting a bug

Email dashgin@praxis.az with:

  • your phone model and its iOS or Android version,
  • the game version (shown in the store listing),
  • what you did and what happened, with a screenshot or screen recording if you can.

Deleting your data

The game keeps no data about you anywhere except on your phone, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything, delete the app. See the privacy policy.
