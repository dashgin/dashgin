About the game

Red Ketch's bandits burned the mill village. You are its Reeve, and old Bram the miller is at your side. You rebuild on a wide map you pan and shape yourself: fields do better by the river, woodcutters by the forest, and roads and decorations lift the buildings beside them. Each level you finish ends a village day, and Dusk brings harvest, upkeep and healing.

Every squad is a villager who leaves the fields. You place up to six squads on a small grid, press Fight, and watch them fight it out live, while you fire three powers yourself: a volley of arrows, a charge, a rally that heals and shields. Pikes stop riders, bows stop pikes, and the game shows who beats whom as you place. Squads that survive won fights earn ranks. A squad that falls is wounded, not killed, and comes back after the next Dusk.

Around every fourth level the game changes shape: lead a crowd of villagers through gates, loose a trebuchet at a siege tower, hold the line at a bridge, or draw the path of a cavalry charge.

Features

  • Your village is your army: one villager makes one squad.
  • Live battles you read at a glance, with three aimed powers and no dice.
  • Strong and Weak counters shown while you place: pikes, bows, blades, riders and levies.
  • A wide village map you shape, with placement bonuses and roads.
  • Five chapters and five bosses who warn you before their big move.
  • Special levels every few fights: crowd, trebuchet, shield wall and charge.
  • Squad ranks, forge upgrades for powers and kit.
  • Free, offline, no ads, no timers. Stars come only from winning.
