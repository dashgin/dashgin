About the game

You play the Reeve, a commoner a river village elected after the valley's last Warden died and three lords started racing for his seat. Each year you build through spring, summer and autumn on a small grid where neighbouring buildings change each other's output. Soldiers are trained from your own farmers, so every recruit is one fewer worker and every loss leaves the village smaller.

Raids and marches play out as side-view, turn-based battles with a front line and a back line. Each enemy shows a red arrow to its target with the exact damage, and you answer by swapping, guarding or striking first. On the valley map, every lord's moves for the year are shown when the year begins, and you choose one march.

Features

  • Soldiers come from your own workforce: every recruit leaves a job empty.
  • Every enemy blow is shown, with its target and damage, before it lands. No dice.
  • Front and back lines across three lanes, and a Blade, Pike, Rider weapon triangle.
  • The Reeve fights in every battle and can call a villager into the line.
  • Eight buildings on a small grid where placement changes output.
  • Seasons instead of timers; winter checks food and firewood.
  • Rival lords whose moves for the year are on the map in advance.
  • Named veterans and stackable charters.
  • First three years free, one purchase for the rest. Plays offline.
